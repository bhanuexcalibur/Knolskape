Part 3 Sales Motion

How To Demo For Outcomes

Demo should sell capability, not features. Here's what a 20-minute enterprise demo looks like.

Min 0-2
The Problem They Own
"Your team is moving to AI-first workflows. But your people aren't ready. Traditional training won't move the needle. You need behavior change in 90 days."
Frame their problem before showing anything
Min 2-7
The Simulation In Action
Show 2-minute clip of participant working through a decision scenario. Don't narrate features. Say: "This isn't a course. They're making real decisions, getting feedback, learning from failure without consequence."
Show, don't explain. Observation is persuasion.
Min 7-12
The Proof Point
Show analytics dashboard. Reference their comparable customer outcomes: time-to-competency improvement, manager assessment scores, retention impact in cohort. Example: "Similar company saw X improve by Y% in Z weeks."
This is where CFO gets convinced. Show customer proof, not invented metrics.
Min 12-18
Scale & Sustainability
Reference similar customer deployments: size of cohort, duration, geographies covered. Explain how platform scales without proportional cost increase. Walk through the coaching feedback workflow and how it maintains quality at scale.
Enterprise wants proof it works at their scale. Show customer examples, not invented numbers.
Min 18-20
The Path Forward
"We typically pilot with 200-500 people over 12 weeks, measure outcomes, then expand. Your first cohort starts in [month]. Here's the deployment timeline."
Move from interest to commitment.
Part 4 Commercial Thinking

From Customer Insight To Product

This role bridges GTM and product. Here's how customer feedback maps to roadmap.

1
Sales Conversation
Prospect: "We need to measure behavior change in the role, not just simulation completion. Your analytics show engagement. We need to show applied impact."
2
GTM Observation
Same objection surfaces in multiple conversations. Enterprise buyers have performance management systems and 360 tools. They want KNOLSKAPE to support demonstration of real-world application.
3
Product Input
Document the gap for product team. Possible roadmap direction: capability to help users demonstrate on-job application of simulated learning. Not inventing a feature, identifying a market need.
4
GTM Messaging Shift
While product evaluates, update battlecards to address the objection. Frame existing capabilities around demonstrating business impact. Become the translator of why this matters to enterprise.
5
Commercial Outcome
Unblock stalled deals through better framing of existing value. Product roadmap is informed by real customer conversation. Both sales and product aligned on market need.
